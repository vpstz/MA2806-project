Tobacco use in the UK

When the UK announced a new smoking ban for anyone born after 2008, it made me wonder how much tobacco use has really changed over the years. How much has smoking actually declined over the years?

This website will be going over how usage has changed over the years, the health effects and the percentage of people trying to quit

Cigarette usage in the UK

Despite health risks, smoking in the UK is very popular, but not as popular as it once was. As shown by the graphs below: back in 2011 around 20% of adults smoked, but this has dropped to around 10% since 2024.

These charts have shown that smoking has gone down dramatically over the last couple of decades: from 30% in 2000 to under 10% since 2024.

An interesting point to note is how the usage by age demographic has changed over the years as well. For the majority of the last two decades a higher percentage of younger Brits smoked compared to the older generations, with 25-34 year olds being having the highest percentage, followed by 16-24 year olds - who managed to overtake the 25-34 year olds a couple oftimes, in 2011 and 2018.

However, starting with 2019, the percentage of people younger than 24 smoking has dropped from being consistently one of the highest percentage of smokers, to now being one of the lowest. With the 25-34 demographic dropping from being the top smokers to now being the 3rd lowest percentage of smokers.

This change in usage by age makes sense as to why the UK government has decided to lockdown on smoking and prevent younger people from smoking, as smoking has already started to decrease in popularity amongst younger people.

The effects of smoking

Awareness around the danger of smoking is increasing, and although most people are aware that it is harmful to you - you probably don't know all the ways it can affect you

It's not just harmful to your body, bit it also impacts your mind/mental health, appearance, finances and relationships. test

Physical affects

Smoking has a plethora of harmful affects on your health, ranging from things that you can notice after smoking for not too long, to life threatening conditions as you age.

Mind and mental health

Most smokers take a cigarette to try and help focus or calm themselves, but it can actually have the opposite effect. It only give you a short burst of nicotine to relax you, before quickly exiting your system and leaving you feeling slightly worse than you start - which can lead to a vicious cycle where you feel like you need to keep taking it otherwise your anxiety and depression could get worse.
