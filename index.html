Cigarette usage in the UK

Despite health risks, smoking in the UK is very popular, but not as popular as it once was. As shown by the graphs below: back in 2011 around 20% of adults smoked, but this has dropped to under 13% since 2012.

This could be due to increased awareness around the dangers of smoking and/or the population moving away from cigarettes to other forms of nicotine like vaping.
